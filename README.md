I am broadly interested in the end-to-end pipeline of precision health—from developing technologies for collecting high-quality data with wearable devices to designing statistically principled and AI-driven methods that deliver precise, personalized health recommendations.

Outside of research, I am an avid runner and an enthusiastic user of wearable technologies. I regularly track my training, sleep, heart rate, recovery, and other physiological signals.
